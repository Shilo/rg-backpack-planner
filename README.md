## What is Backpack Planner?

Backpack Planner helps you plan and optimize your Backpack Tech builds for *Run! Goddess*. Whether you’re leveling Guardian, Vanguard, or Cannon trees, you can experiment with different setups, track your Tech Crystal costs, and share builds with friends—all in one place.

---

## Features

### Plan Your Builds

- **Interactive skill trees** — Tap or click nodes to level skills up and down. Pan and zoom to explore the full tree.
- **All three trees** — Guardian, Vanguard, and Cannon in one app. Switch between them with a tap.
- **Smart leveling** — Levels sync along connected paths so you can quickly adjust entire branches.
- **Leaf cap** — At most 3 leaf nodes can be leveled at once, matching the game’s rules.

### Track Your Progress

- **Tech Crystal tracking** — Enter how many crystals you own and see how much each build costs.
- **Statistics** — View totals per tree and overall. Copy stats to the clipboard when you need them.
- **Visual feedback** — See which nodes are locked, available, maxed, or active at a glance.

### Save and Organize

- **Multiple builds** — Save several named presets and switch between them instantly.
- **Auto-save** — Your active build is saved automatically. No need to hit save.
- **Premade builds** — Try starter, early, mid, and late-game builds to get ideas.

### Share With Others

- **Share links** — Generate a link that encodes your full build. Anyone with the link can view it.
- **Native share** — Use your device’s share sheet when available, or copy the link to the clipboard.
- **Screenshot export** — Capture all three trees in one image to share on social or in guides.
- **Preview mode** — Open a shared link or paste a build code to view a build without overwriting your own presets. Clone it into your presets when you like it.

### Works Everywhere

- **Installable** — Add to your home screen and use it like a native app.
- **Offline** — Works without internet after the first load.
- **Responsive** — Optimized for phones, tablets, and desktops.
- **Themes** — Choose light or dark mode, or pick a custom accent color (cyan, blue, green, rose, amber, neutral).
- **Languages** — English, Japanese, and Chinese supported.

## Settings

Open the side menu to customize how the app behaves:

### Node Behavior

- **Primary action** — When you tap or left-click a node, choose whether it adds +1, +10, or +Tier level(s).
- **Level sync** — *Sync Lineage* (default) updates connected nodes when you change one. *Solo Only* changes only the node you tap.

### View

- **Tier badge** — Show or hide the tier number on each node.
- **Skill name** — Show or hide the skill name on each node.
- **Tree zoom** — Start with the whole tree in view (*Fit*) or zoomed in for larger nodes (*Close-up*).
- **Font size** — Adjust text size for labels and UI.

### Look and Feel

- **Theme** — Light or dark mode.
- **Accent color** — Pick a preset or choose a custom color.
- **Haptic feedback** — Vibration when tapping nodes (on supported devices).
- **Fullscreen** — Toggle fullscreen mode where your browser supports it.

### Data

- **Reset tree** — Refund Tech Crystals and set all nodes in the current tree (or all trees) back to level 0.
- **Reset settings** — Restore all settings to defaults. Does not affect your builds.
- **Clear all data** — Delete all presets, settings, and progress, then reload the app.

## Controls

### Mobile (touch)

- **Tap** a node — Add level(s) based on your primary action setting.
- **Long-press** a node — Open the node menu (+1, +10, +Tier, -1, -10, Reset, Max).
- **Long-press** empty space or a tab — Open the tree menu (reset, focus in view, share, preview).
- **Drag** with one finger — Pan around the tree.
- **Pinch** with two fingers — Zoom in and out.
- **Swipe right** on the side menu — Close the menu.

### PC (mouse and keyboard)

- **Left-click** a node — Add level(s) based on your primary action setting.
- **Shift + left-click** or **middle-click** a node — Remove level(s).
- **Right-click** a node — Open the node menu (+1, +10, +Tier, -1, -10, Reset, Max).
- **Right-click** empty space or a tab — Open the tree menu (reset, focus in view, share, preview).
- **Click and drag** — Pan around the tree.
- **Scroll wheel** or **trackpad** — Zoom in and out.
- **F9** — Open the screenshot composer.
- **Tab** — Cycle between trees, or between menu tabs (Statistics, Settings, Controls).
- **Backspace** — Reset the active tree (when it has levels).
- **Escape** — Open or close the side menu.
